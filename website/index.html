The problem

Receipts, invoices, and paperwork end up scattered across folders, chats, and camera rolls. When you need to review a month or share records, everything feels messy and slow.

The solution

RecordPlus gives you a lightweight workflow: capture documents quickly, add structured details, organize by category and month, then export professional reports when needed — without complex accounting software.

How it works

A simple four-step workflow from capture to export.

  1. 1

    Scan or upload

    Capture via camera, pick from gallery, or upload a PDF document.

  2. 2

    Add details

    Enter name, category, date, and optional amount, currency, and notes.

  3. 3

    Browse by month

    Use profiles, categories, and monthly snapshots to find records fast.

  4. 4

    Export & share

    Generate ZIP packages, financial statements, or document registers.

Frequently asked questions

Is RecordPlus accounting software?

No. RecordPlus is a lightweight document management app for capture, organization, and export. It is not a full accounting or bookkeeping system.

Where is my data stored?

On your device. RecordPlus uses local-first storage and does not automatically upload your documents to external servers.

Which languages are supported?

RecordPlus supports English, Malay, Chinese, and Indonesian, with light and dark theme options.

Which platforms are supported?

RecordPlus is a mobile app designed for iOS and Android. Store links will be available here when the app launches.
